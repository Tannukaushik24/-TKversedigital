FAQ

Questions Before You Ask.

How much does digital marketing cost?

Most retainers sit between $800 and $3,000 per month depending on channels and ad spend. Ad budget is paid directly to the platform, never to us. We quote a fixed scope after the free audit so there is no ambiguity.

How long before we see results?

Paid advertising can move within two to four weeks. SEO typically takes three to six months to compound. We set expectations for both during the strategy call rather than after the invoice.

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. We start on a 90-day initial term because that is the honest window for testing, then roll into monthly terms. If we are not earning the retainer, you leave.

Who actually works on our account?

A senior strategist and a specialist in the channels you need. You get a direct line to both, plus full access to every ad account and dashboard we touch.

What do you need from us to get started?

Read access to your analytics and ad accounts, your customer margin numbers so we can bid on profit, and roughly three hours a month from whoever can approve creative.
